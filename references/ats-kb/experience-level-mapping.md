# Experience Level Mapping

| Toplam Deneyim | Tipik Seviye | Yaygın Unvanlar |
|----------------|--------------|-----------------|
| 0-2 yıl | Junior | Junior Engineer, Analyst, Associate |
| 2-5 yıl | Mid | Engineer, Specialist, Product Manager |
| 5-8 yıl | Senior | Senior Engineer, Senior PM, Lead IC |
| 8+ yıl | Lead / Manager | Lead, Staff, Principal, Manager, Head |
